The idea is two-sided: retired tradespeople (mentors) and apprentices.
1. The reply recognises both sides, in its own words.
2. It asks, or raises as a gentle challenge with a reason and an alternative, how the
   app works before both sides are there: which side comes first, how the first
   apprentice gets value when there are few mentors (or the reverse). This is the
   cold-start question, and it must not be silently assumed away.
3. It asks at most 3 questions, and none of them is about pricing, the platform's cut,
   the app's name or the services and hosting to use.
