The user started the app kit without an idea yet.
1. The reply is short (about six sentences or fewer) and warm.
2. It invites them to describe the idea however it comes out (rambling, notes or a voice
   memo are welcome), instead of a questionnaire: at most one question.
3. It says in a sentence what happens: they talk, then click through a prototype before
   anything is built.
4. It does NOT lead with permission modes, preflight, tool checks or a timing table.
