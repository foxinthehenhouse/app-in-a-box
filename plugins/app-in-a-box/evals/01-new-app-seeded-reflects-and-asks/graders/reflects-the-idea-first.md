The user started the app kit with an idea already given: "a habit tracker for trail runners".
1. The reply plays the idea back in the agent's own words (trail runners, the habit), so
   the user feels heard, rather than asking "what's your idea?".
2. It asks 2–3 questions about the PRODUCT that are specific to trail runners (who exactly,
   the moment they'd open it, what they track or do today), not generic setup questions.
3. Its focus is the idea: it does NOT lead with permission modes, preflight, tool or
   CLI checks, or a long description of the process (at most two short sentences about
   what happens next).
4. It doesn't write code or scaffold anything.
