The reply asks at most 3 questions in total (count every question the user is expected
to answer, whether numbered, in a structured question tool, or inline; a closing "say if
any of that is wrong" counts as one at most). The questions are about the product
(who exactly, the moment they reach for it, what they do today, what they should feel
in the first session, who else is in it, how the first users find it). It does NOT ask
about the app's name or bundle ID, pricing or a paywall, the store listing, or which
services and hosting to use: those wait for later phases.
