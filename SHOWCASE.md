## Add yours

Two ways, whichever is easier:

1. Open a [Show your app](https://github.com/foxinthehenhouse/app-in-a-box/issues/new?template=show-your-app.yml)
   issue. A maintainer adds the line.
2. Send a PR that adds one line under **Apps**, newest first:
   `- **[App name](https://link)**: one line about it. *Stage* · built with Claude Code`

A few ground rules, so the list stays useful:

- Prototype, in testing, live in a store or open source: all welcome. Say which.
- A link is optional. Only add links you're happy to make public.
- Describe it in your own words. Plain is better than polished.
- Apps that break store rules or the law are removed.

Apps generated with v0.7.0 or later start with a small "Built with App in a Box"
badge in their README. Keeping it helps other people find the kit; deleting it is
fine too.
